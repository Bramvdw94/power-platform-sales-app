How the Quote PDF looks and how a Sent Quote reaches the customer

Example Quote Q-00012, revision 1, for Fabrikam Logistiek B.V. Every name and amount is sample data. The sheet stays white in dark mode because it is the printed document.

Layout

Recommended: Send opens one dialog with two ways to deliver
  • Default: email the PDF from the user's own mailbox through the Office 365 Outlook connector. It lands in their Sent Items, so replies come back to them.
  • Alternative: download the PDF and send it yourself (another mail client, a portal, a printed copy).
  • Both lock the Quote, set it to Sent, record the sent date and store the exact PDF on the Quote.
  • This is a one-off send, not Outlook integration: no email activity is tracked, so the "no Outlook" scope line still holds.

Subject and message come from the template in Quote settings; the user can change them before sending. To is the Quote's Contact; the dialog stops with a message if that Contact has no email address.

Recommended: the Quote page shows what was sent, to whom, and the exact PDF
  • A copy of the sent PDF is stored on the Quote, so it never changes later, even when prices or company details do.
  • "Sent" line: date, who sent it, how (Outlook or download) and to which address.
  • Actions on a Sent Quote: Accept, Reject, Revise. Download PDF always returns the stored copy.
  • A Draft has "Preview PDF" only, with a DRAFT watermark and no stored copy.
